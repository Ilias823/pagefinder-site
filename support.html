Contact

Email spoons-creek-8u@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, PageFinder version and what happened. You can find the PageFinder version in Settings, under About.

Supported files

  • Audiobooks: one .m4b, .m4a or .mp3 file, or a folder or .zip file of chapter files.
  • Ebooks: .epub files without copy protection.

Add files with the + button, share them to PageFinder from another app, or copy them into the PageFinder folder in the Files app.

Copy-protected audiobooks

PageFinder can't read copy-protected audiobooks, including titles from Audible and Apple Books, or protected .aa, .aax, .aaxc and .m4p files. Copy-protected ebooks can't be read either.

DRM-free audiobooks work, for example from Libro.fm, Downpour or LibriVox.

Languages

PageFinder currently supports English audiobooks and English book pages. You can choose English (US) or English (UK) in Settings.

First-time speech download

The first time you prepare an audiobook, iOS may need an internet connection to download Apple's English speech model. If it can't be downloaded yet, PageFinder shows a message and tries again later. You can also tap Try again on the book's Page search screen.

Your audiobook isn't uploaded. Transcription runs on your iPhone.
