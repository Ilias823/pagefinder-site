PageFinder Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

In short

PageFinder has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer doesn't receive your books, photos or reading activity.

Your audiobooks and ebooks

Files you add are kept in PageFinder on your iPhone. Audiobooks are transcribed on your iPhone with Apple's speech recognition, and the audio isn't uploaded. The first time, iOS may download Apple's English speech model.

Page photos

Photos and scans of pages are read on your iPhone to find your place. PageFinder doesn't save them. Text it needs, such as chapter names and page numbers from a contents page, stays with the book.

Camera and photo access

The camera is used only when you scan a page. When you choose a picture, iOS shows you the picker and PageFinder receives only the picture you select.

Backups

Your library details, listening positions, transcripts and ebook text can be included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, under your own account. Audiobook audio is left out of backups.

Deleting your data

Delete a book from your shelf to remove it and everything PageFinder learned about it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Links

Links in Settings open in your web browser, where that website's own privacy policy applies.

Contact

Questions about privacy: pagefindersupport@gmail.com.
